# Resolution speed by agency

Agencies with at least 1,000 requests in the window, ordered by volume. Means are rebuilt from their components rather than averaged across months, because averaging a ratio of ratios weights a quiet month the same as a busy one.

The two mean columns are different quantities and the gap between them is the censoring bias for that agency, not a data problem.
